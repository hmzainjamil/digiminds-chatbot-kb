# Content review register

Reviewed 2026-10-02 against the tracked content files. This register records documentation gaps; it does not validate the business claims.

| Content area | Status | Required evidence or action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business profile and contact details | Needs owner review | Confirm public business identity, contact channels, office details, service scope, and update date | Business owner |
| Client results, case studies, guarantees, and benchmarks | Unverified | Provide dated source records and approval for each claim; remove unsupported claims | Business and legal/compliance owners |
| Certifications, partnerships, and platform integrations | Unverified | Confirm current status with authoritative records; distinguish certification from implied affiliation | Business owner |
| Pricing and commercial terms | Unverified | Approve current offer, scope, currency, terms, and disclosure | Commercial owner |
| Competitor matrix | Unverified | Cite dated public sources; remove unsupported comparative claims | Marketing owner |
| Psychographic segments | Needs privacy review | Remove sensitive inference; validate necessity, fairness, and user choice | Privacy owner |
| Sensitive-topic playbook | Needs specialist review | Review regulated-topic boundaries and human escalation with qualified reviewers | Legal/compliance owner |
| Conversation flows and conversion logic | Needs privacy review | Remove pressure tactics; approve consent, minimization, opt-out, and retention behavior | Product/privacy owner |
| PDF exports | Stale until compared | Rebuild from approved source and inspect text and rendered pages | Documentation owner |
| Deployment proposal | Proposal only | Replace with a selected, documented, tested architecture after approvals | Technical owner |

No chatbot runtime or evaluation artifacts exist in the tracked tree. The automated assistant prompt was revised to prohibit unsupported claims, pressure, and non-consensual contact collection. That prompt change alone does not validate or sanitize all KB data, PDFs, or future deployments.
